PaperTrail Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

The short version. PaperTrail's AI runs on your phone. The papers you add, the questions you ask and the answers you get stay on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else. The only information we hold is what you give us to create an account: your name and email address.

This policy explains what information the PaperTrail app ("PaperTrail", "we", "us") handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the PaperTrail app for Android and iOS.

Information that stays on your device

The following is created and stored only on your phone. We never receive, upload, see or share it:

  • the PDF papers you add and the text extracted from them;
  • the search index built from your papers;
  • your projects, questions, answers and conversation history;
  • the AI models, once they're downloaded.

Answers are written by an AI model that runs on your device. Your papers and questions are not sent to any server to produce them.

Information we collect

To create and sign in to your account, we collect:

  • Your name, which you enter when you sign up;
  • Your email address, used to sign you in, verify your account and reset your password;
  • Your password, which is stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider and is never visible to us;
  • Whether you agreed to receive product emails (research tips, product updates and new features). We only send these if you opt in.

Like any online service, the servers that handle sign-in receive technical information that comes with every connection, such as your IP address and the time of the request. This is used to keep the service working and secure.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores your account (name, email, hashed password and email preference) and handles sign-in. See the Supabase privacy policy.
  • Hugging Face hosts the AI models. When the app downloads a model, your device connects to Hugging Face, which receives the standard information of any download, such as your IP address. No account details, papers or questions are sent. See the Hugging Face privacy policy.

PaperTrail has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it except with the services above, which process it for us, or when the law requires it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, PaperTrail can tell you when your papers have finished indexing or an answer is ready, and shows a notification while it works in the background. These are created on your device; no notification content is sent through our servers.

How long we keep information

We keep your account information for as long as you have an account. When your account is deleted, we delete your name, email address and password from our authentication provider. Information on your device stays until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Data on your device: in the app, go to Profile, then Clear all research data, to delete every project, paper, index and conversation. Uninstalling the app also removes it.
  • Product emails: use the unsubscribe link in any email, or email us.
  • Your account: request deletion in the app or by email; see below.

Deleting your account

You can ask us to delete your account in either of two ways:

  • In the app: go to Profile, then Delete account. This also removes every project, paper and conversation on your phone and signs you out.
  • By email: write to mdcccxv@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". You don't need the app installed.

Within 30 days we delete your account and everything we hold about you: your name, email address, password and email preference. Data on your device is never sent to us; remove it in the app or by uninstalling.

Security

Account information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and your password is stored hashed. Because your papers and questions never leave your device, they are protected by your phone's own security, such as its screen lock and encryption.

Children

PaperTrail is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or by email.
